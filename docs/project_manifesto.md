# Stockball Stock — Project Manifesto

## 1. Mission

Stockball Stock is a research project built around a simple question:

> **Can repeatable statistical patterns in financial markets identify situations with a high probability of positive returns while prioritizing the preservation of capital?**

The project is inspired by the philosophy behind *Stockball*: individual statistics may provide limited information, but combinations of carefully selected statistics may reveal patterns that conventional observation misses.

The objective is not to predict the market perfectly.

The objective is to **identify measurable situations where historical evidence suggests the odds have shifted in our favor.**

---

## 2. Core Philosophy

Stockball Stock follows three priorities, in this order:

### 1. Protect Capital

Avoiding unnecessary losses is more important than maximizing potential returns.

### 2. Increase Probability

Prefer repeatable situations with a high historical probability of positive outcomes.

### 3. Pursue Return

Only after risk and probability are acceptable do we consider the potential size of the return.

Therefore:

> **Capital Preservation → Probability → Return**

A smaller, more dependable opportunity may be preferable to a larger but significantly less predictable opportunity.

---

## 3. Small Edges, Repeated

Stockball Stock is not designed to search for jackpots, meme stocks, lottery-ticket trades, or extraordinary short-term returns.

It searches for **small statistical advantages that may be repeatable**.

A 1% return is not a daily requirement or prediction.

Instead, **1% represents a philosophy**:

> Do not become greedy. Find favorable situations, take reasonable returns when available, protect capital, and allow repeated small advantages to accumulate over time.

There may be periods where no suitable opportunity exists.

That is acceptable.

---

## 4. WAIT Is a Position

The system is never required to recommend a trade.

If the statistical evidence is weak, contradictory, unusual, or insufficient:

> **WAIT.**

Cash is a valid position.

One of Stockball Stock's most important potential advantages is the ability to do nothing without boredom, fear of missing out, or emotional pressure.

A day without a trade is not a failed day.

A week without a trade is not a failed week.

The system participates only when evidence justifies participation.

---

## 5. Evidence Over Intuition

Ideas and intuition are welcome.

They generate hypotheses.

They do not validate them.

Every meaningful hypothesis must eventually face historical evidence.

Instead of asking:

> "Does this look like a good opportunity?"

Stockball asks:

> **"When approximately this situation occurred previously, what happened next?"**

The answer may confirm the hypothesis.

It may reject it.

Both outcomes are useful.

---

## 6. Patterns, Not Predictions

Stockball Stock does not attempt to predict the exact future price of an asset.

Markets contain uncertainty.

Instead, the project searches for relationships between measurable conditions and subsequent outcomes.

Examples may include combinations of:

* broader market regime
* sector strength
* momentum
* trend
* relative strength
* volume
* volatility
* price behavior
* drawdowns
* macroeconomic conditions
* fundamentals
* catalysts
* correlations
* historical analogues

No individual indicator should be treated as magical.

The primary research question is whether **multiple useful signals can be stacked to produce stronger statistical evidence than any individual signal provides alone.**

---

## 7. Market → Sector → Asset

Where appropriate, Stockball analyzes opportunities from the outside inward:

> **Market → Sector → Asset → Setup → Historical Match → Risk → Decision**

The broader environment matters.

An asset showing momentum during a strong market and strong sector may represent a fundamentally different statistical situation from the same asset showing identical momentum during a deteriorating market.

Context is part of the pattern.

---

## 8. Probability Before Profit

A large historical average return is not enough.

Stockball prioritizes understanding:

* How frequently did the pattern succeed?
* How frequently did it fail?
* How large were typical losses?
* How severe were unusual losses?
* How far did the asset move against the position?
* Did the pattern survive difficult markets?
* How many historical examples exist?
* Does the apparent advantage persist outside the period in which it was discovered?

Only after answering those questions should potential return become a major consideration.

> **We do not first ask how much we could make. We first ask how likely we are to lose and how much we could lose.**

---

## 9. Different Markets Must Be Respected

A pattern discovered during one type of market cannot automatically be assumed to work in another.

Stockball should eventually distinguish between environments such as:

* bull markets
* bear markets
* corrections
* recoveries
* high-volatility markets
* low-volatility markets
* rising-rate environments
* falling-rate environments
* recessions
* expansions

Long historical datasets are valuable because they expose hypotheses to environments radically different from the present.

Patterns that survive multiple regimes deserve greater confidence than patterns dependent on one unusual period.

---

## 10. Failed Experiments Are Successful Research

Stockball Stock must never become a machine for proving our own ideas correct.

If an experiment finds no meaningful relationship, that is a valid result.

If a promising hypothesis performs poorly, record it.

If an indicator contributes no useful information, remove it from future consideration.

If a cherished theory fails, abandon the theory.

> **The purpose of an experiment is to discover what is true, not to produce an attractive result.**

---

## 11. Experiments Are Immutable

Every experiment must define its hypothesis, variables, methodology, comparison group, evaluation period, and primary measurements **before its results are examined**.

Once results have been generated, the original experiment is frozen.

Its rules are not modified to improve its performance.

If the results inspire a modification, that modification becomes a **new experiment**.

For example:

`Experiment 001 → Result → New hypothesis → Experiment 002`

Never:

`Experiment 001 → Bad result → Change rules → Better result → Pretend this was Experiment 001`

This rule exists to protect the project from unconscious overfitting.

---

## 12. Statistical Honesty

Stockball must actively defend itself against misleading results.

Research should account for problems including:

* look-ahead bias
* survivorship bias
* selection bias
* data leakage
* overfitting
* insufficient sample sizes
* repeated hypothesis testing
* regime dependence
* unrealistic execution assumptions
* transaction costs
* spreads
* slippage

A beautiful backtest is not evidence of a useful strategy unless the methodology that produced it is credible.

---

## 13. Sample Size Matters

A pattern that succeeded 9 times out of 10 may be interesting.

A pattern that succeeded 6,300 times out of 10,000 may be far more important.

Stockball should always consider both:

> **Probability + Evidence**

Confidence should increase when a pattern appears repeatedly across many observations, assets, periods, and market environments.

Rare patterns may still be investigated, but uncertainty must be acknowledged.

---

## 14. The Research Comes Before the Product

Stockball Stock begins as a research laboratory.

The initial objective is not to build an attractive stock-picking application.

The initial objective is to determine whether useful patterns exist.

The software exists to help us:

**Collect → Measure → Test → Compare → Record → Learn**

Only when the research process itself becomes sufficiently understood should a larger application be built around it.

The interface must serve the methodology.

The methodology must never be distorted to serve the interface.

---

## 15. Automation Removes Emotion, Not Responsibility

One potential advantage of software is consistency.

Software does not become:

* greedy
* frightened
* impatient
* bored
* euphoric
* attached to a previous decision

Stockball should use automation to enforce research rules and consistently evaluate evidence.

However, automation does not make a conclusion correct.

Human oversight, skepticism, and risk management remain essential.

---

## 16. No Real Capital Until the Evidence Earns It

Early experiments are research.

Backtests are research.

Simulations are research.

Paper trading is research.

None automatically justify risking real capital.

Progress should occur gradually:

> **Historical Research → Validation → Out-of-Sample Testing → Paper Trading → Extended Observation → Small Real-World Testing**

Each stage must earn progression to the next.

There is no deadline.

---

## 17. Reproducibility

Every important result should be reproducible.

For every experiment, we should be able to determine:

* what data was used
* where that data came from
* what period was tested
* which assets were included
* what calculations were performed
* what parameters were selected
* what assumptions were made
* what costs were assumed
* what results were produced

Months or years later, another researcher should theoretically be able to reconstruct the experiment and obtain approximately the same result.

---

## 18. The Stockball Question

Every experiment ultimately attempts to answer some variation of:

> **When these conditions existed together in the past, what tended to happen next?**

If the answer repeatedly demonstrates:

**high probability + controlled downside + sufficient evidence**

then we may have discovered something worth investigating further.

If not, we keep searching.

---

# The Stockball Rule

When uncertainty exists, return to this hierarchy:

> ## Protect Capital.
>
> ## Improve the Odds.
>
> ## Take the Return.
>
> ## Never Force the Trade.

We are not trying to be right every time.

We are trying to determine whether disciplined analysis can identify moments when **the odds are sufficiently in our favor to justify taking the risk.**
